Privacy Policy

How YENYONG collects, uses and protects your personal data on yenyong.com.

1. Who we are

YENYONG PTE. LTD. ("YENYONG", "we", "us") is a building-materials exporter registered in Singapore, with manufacturing partners based in Foshan, China. This policy explains what personal data we collect through yenyong.com and how we use it.

2. What we collect

When you contact us or request free samples, we collect the details you choose to provide: your name, company name, email address, phone number, country and inquiry content. Our server logs also record standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance purposes.

3. How we use it

We use your information solely to: respond to inquiries, prepare quotations, arrange sample delivery, and — where you have opted in — send occasional product updates. We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties. Data is shared only with logistics and certification partners strictly as needed to fulfil your request.

4. Cookies

This website uses a minimal cookie notice and does not deploy third-party advertising trackers. You may clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site remains fully functional.

5. Data retention & your rights

Inquiry data is retained for up to 24 months. You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing sales@yenyong.com. We respond to all verified requests within 30 days.

6. Contact

Privacy questions: sales@yenyong.com · YENYONG PTE. LTD., Singapore.
