Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

1. Data Controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Foshan YenYong International Building Materials Trading Co., Ltd. ("YENYONG.", "we", "us") and its affiliate KYT Trading & Construction Pte Ltd (Singapore) collect, use and protect personal data when you visit yenyong.com or contact us for quotations, samples or partnership.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact data you provide: name, company, email, phone, country, and message content submitted through our inquiry form, email or WhatsApp.
  • Project data: product interests, quantities, destination port, and technical requirements you share with us.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, pages visited and approximate location, collected via privacy-friendly analytics and essential cookies.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to inquiries, prepare quotations and arrange samples within 24 hours.
  • To fulfil orders, manage logistics and provide after-sales support.
  • To improve our website, products and services.
  • To comply with legal, tax and trade obligations.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For visitors in the European Economic Area, we rely on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) to respond to your requests, and consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for non-essential cookies and marketing communications, which you may withdraw at any time.

5. Sharing & International Transfer

Your data may be shared with logistics partners, factories and certification bodies strictly necessary to serve your request. As a Singapore-registered group operating from Foshan, China, data may be transferred internationally under appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses where required). We never sell your data.

6. WhatsApp & Third Parties

If you contact us via WhatsApp, your communication is processed by Meta Platforms under its own privacy policy. We use it solely to serve your inquiry.

7. Data Retention

We keep inquiry and order records for the period required by tax, trade and contract law (generally up to 7 years), and remove marketing data when no longer needed or upon request.

8. Your Rights

  • Access, rectify or erase your personal data.
  • Restrict or object to processing, and request data portability.
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies for site function and, with your consent, analytics cookies. You can manage preferences via the cookie banner or your browser settings.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
