Colour Trends 2026: Neutral Tones and Stone Looks Dominate Specs

By YENYONG Technical Team · 2026-03-22

This season’s specification requests lean toward restraint: warm neutrals, soft greys, and realistic stone and terrazzo looks — with wood tones moving lighter and bleached.

The palette shift

  • Warm neutrals — sand, clay, ivory bamboo replacing cool greys.
  • Stone & terrazzo — Calacatta, Statuario and terrazzo mist for large-format tiles and panels.
  • Bleached woods — sun-bleached oak and maple for calmer interiors.
  • Mirror & matte UV — high-gloss statement walls balanced by matte surrounds.
Material libraries that lead with 12–20 curated neutrals convert better than 200 unfiltered SKUs.

Building your 2026 library

YENYONG’s colour library groups every decor by family (Wood / Stone / Solid / Mirror / Matte) so specifiers can build a coherent palette fast — and request samples by code.
