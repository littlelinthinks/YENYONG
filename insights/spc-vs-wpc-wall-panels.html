SPC vs WPC Wall Panels: Which Should Your Project Specify?

By YENYONG Technical Team · 2026-01-18

On paper, SPC and WPC wall panels look interchangeable. In practice, choosing the wrong one for a commercial wall can cost a project weeks and a full re-order. This guide breaks down what actually matters.

Core composition

WPC blends wood fibre with PVC — lighter, warmer to the touch, with better sound absorption, ideal for vertical wall cladding. SPC replaces the wood with limestone powder, producing a denser, more rigid, fully waterproof panel.

Where each wins

  • WPC — interior feature walls, reception backsplashes, corridors where acoustic comfort matters.
  • SPC — wet zones (bathroom walls, kitchen splashbacks), high-traffic commercial walls, areas needing B1 fire rating and easy cleaning.
Rule of thumb for B2B: specify SPC for walls that meet water or heavy cleaning; specify WPC for dry decorative walls where natural aesthetics and lightness lead.
