The Buyer’s Guide to Flooring & Wall Panel Certifications

By YENYONG Technical Team · 2026-02-02

For importers and contractors, a certificate is only useful if it names the test body and ties to the production line. Here is what to look for.

Must-have for EU / UK

  • CE Marking + Declaration of Performance — legal baseline for construction products in the EU.
  • EN 13501-1 — reaction to fire classification (aim for B or better for commercial walls).
  • REACH — confirms restricted substances are controlled.
  • FloorScore / Indoor Air Comfort — low VOC, better for occupied spaces.

Quality management

ISO 9001 (quality) and ISO 14001 (environmental) signal a manufacturer with controlled processes — important for repeat orders and consistent colour.

A credible supplier explains how a product fails under wrong use. That is worth more than a universal "passes everything" claim.

What to request from a manufacturer

  • Test reports from SGS / TÜV / an EU notified body, naming the exact SKU.
  • Batch-to-batch colour stability data.
  • EPD (Environmental Product Declaration) for green-building credits.
