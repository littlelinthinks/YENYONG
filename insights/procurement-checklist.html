    WPC vs PVC vs SPC vs Solid Wood: A Procurement Manager’s Checklist

    By YENYONG Technical Team · 2026-03-05

    When you are buying for fifty locations, design value is only one of ten factors. This checklist keeps procurement decisions consistent.

    Evaluate across the full buying cycle

    FactorWhat to check
    MoistureGrade for the actual zone; SPC/PVC lead in wet areas.
    Design repeatabilityColour consistency across batches and sites.
    Installation speedClick vs glue; crew learning curve.
    DocumentationSpec sheet, certs, install guide, replacement policy.
    SupplyMOQ, lead time, packaging, loading quantity.

    Common failure modes

    • PVC dents or looks cheap in premium lobbies.
    • WPC warps if formulation or installation is weak.
    • SPC chips if mishandled.
    • Solid wood moves and needs refinishing.
    Treat a wall-panel line as a sellable channel system — not a one-time decorative item.

    Ask your supplier for colour cards, trims and repeat-order support, not just panels.
