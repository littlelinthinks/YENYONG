    Why Distributors Are Shifting to Stone-Composite (SPC) for Commercial Flooring

    By YENYONG Technical Team · 2026-02-20

    According to Grand View Research, the global SPC flooring market reached USD 5.93 billion in 2025, with commercial applications growing at ~7.6% CAGR. The driver is not residential DIY — it is professional specifiers.

    Three reasons commercial buyers choose SPC

    • Durability under load — the rigid stone core handles rolling loads and foot traffic that LVT cannot.
    • Waterproofing by chemistry — zero wood content means no swelling in wet cleaning regimes.
    • Speed — click-lock installation reduces labour time on large-scale fit-outs.
    Lifecycle cost — not the sticker price — is the metric that wins commercial specifications.

    What distributors should stock

    A focused decor range (12–20 wood and stone looks), consistent colour cards, and a spec sheet that names the wear layer, plank size and fire class. YENYONG’s colour library is organised exactly this way.
