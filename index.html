Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SPC and laminate flooring?
SPC has a stone-plastic composite core, making it 100% waterproof and dimensionally stable. Laminate uses a wood-fiber core that swells when wet, so it is not suitable for bathrooms or wet areas.
Does SPC flooring need underlayment and expansion gaps?
Most SPC products include an attached IXPE underlay. On very uneven subfloors you can add a thin foam layer. Always leave an 8–10 mm expansion gap around walls and vertical obstructions.
Can WPC wall panels be used in bathrooms?
Yes. WPC is water-resistant and handles normal humidity well. For shower zones, ensure good ventilation and seal panel joints with a suitable silicone.
How do I clean and maintain crystal panels?
Use a soft cloth with neutral detergent. Avoid abrasive pads, steel wool and strong solvents. Dust regularly and wipe spills promptly to protect the UV surface.
Will different production batches vary in color?
Minor batch variation is normal in all manufactured panels. For one project, order all material in a single batch and we will batch-match every project order at the factory.
What if goods arrive damaged or short?
Photograph the cartons and contents at unloading and note any damage or shortage on the delivery receipt. Contact us within 48 hours with the photos — our after-sales team handles every claim personally.
Do you provide installation training or on-site support?
Every order ships with illustrated installation guides, and our team supports installers by WhatsApp or video call. On-site supervision is available for large hotel or commercial projects.
What warranty do you offer?
Warranty coverage depends on the product line and project type. Contact us for the warranty policy that applies to your product — full documents are available on request.

Frequently asked questions

What products does YENYONG supply?

YENYONG supplies factory-direct building materials from Foshan — SPC rigid-core flooring, WPC and PVC wall panels, bamboo-crystal and carbon-crystal panels, ceramic & porcelain tiles, acoustic panels, honeycomb panels and aluminium-plastic panels for flooring, wall cladding and interior decoration.

What is the difference between SPC and WPC wall panels?

SPC (stone-plastic composite) wall panels are rigid, waterproof and impact-resistant — ideal for high-traffic and wet areas. WPC (wood-plastic composite) panels are lighter and warmer to the touch, better for residential interiors. Both are CE-certified and ship from our Foshan factory.
