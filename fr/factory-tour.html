Virtual Factory Tour

See where YENYONG. products are manufactured — from raw material to finished goods.

Our manufacturing partners operate modern production facilities across Guangdong, China. Below is a visual walkthrough of the typical production process for wall panel and flooring products.

Production Process

Stage 1

Raw Material Warehouse

SPC compound, PVC decorative film, MDF substrate and aluminium coil are stored in climate-controlled warehouses with batch traceability.

Stage 2

Extrusion / Pressing

SPC cores are extruded to precise thickness; wood-based panels are hot-pressed with decorative film and wear layer.

Stage 3

Lamination

Decorative film is applied under controlled temperature and pressure for consistent adhesion and registered embossing.

Stage 4

Cutting & Sizing

Panels are cut to specification with precision saws; edges are trimmed and profiled for click-lock or tongue-and-groove systems.

Stage 5

QC Inspection

Every batch undergoes dimensional, visual and performance testing before packing. Defective units are removed.

Stage 6

Packing & Palletisation

Panels are packed in protective cartons, palletised and stretch-wrapped for container shipping.

Stage 7

Finished Goods Warehouse

Completed orders are stored in labelled bays awaiting container loading and PSI inspection.

Stage 8

Container Loading

Loading is supervised by our QC team with photo documentation and seal verification.
