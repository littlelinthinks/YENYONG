Brand Guidelines

Visual identity standards for all YENYONG. marketing materials, digital assets and communications.

1. Logo Usage

The YENYONG. logo consists of the wordmark "YENYONG" in uppercase with a trailing period, and an orange accent ribbon element.

RuleSpecification
Minimum size24px height (digital) / 10mm height (print)
Clear spaceMinimum 1x the height of the "Y" around all sides
BackgroundWhite, light blue (#E8EEFF), or dark (#0A0E1A) only
ProportionsNever stretch, skew, or rotate the logo
ColourFull colour on light backgrounds; white on dark backgrounds

✓ Do: Use the original logo file, maintain proportions, use on approved backgrounds.

✗ Don't: Change colours, add effects, place on busy images, or alter the orange ribbon.
