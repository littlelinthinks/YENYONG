Questions about this product

Questions about this product

Is the mirror finish as fragile as real glass?
No. It is a polished composite surface, not a glass laminate — it will not shatter. It can still scratch under sharp impact, so handle with the protective film on until final fitting.
Where should I not use carbon crystal panels?
Outdoor exposure and continuously wet areas. These are interior decorative panels; for exterior or permanently wet locations, ask us about the right product instead.
How do I avoid visible seams on a mirror feature wall?
Plan the layout from the room centre outward, use matched joint profiles, and keep panels in one batch. Mirror finishes show alignment errors more than matte, so set out carefully before fixing.
Can I get a sample before ordering?
Yes — samples are free; you only cover courier cost. Request a sample pack and we will send the finishes you are considering.
