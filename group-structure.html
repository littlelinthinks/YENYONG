YENYONG. Group Structure

Singapore-headquartered contracting with Foshan-based procurement and a verified network of Chinese manufacturing partners.

YENYONG. operates as a three-tier organisation: international contracting in Singapore, procurement and quality control in Foshan, and manufacturing through audited partner factories across Guangdong. This structure gives clients the legal security of a Singapore contract with the cost advantage of direct China sourcing.

How We Work

Step 1

Singapore Contract

All orders are contracted through OVERARCHING PTE. LTD. in Singapore, providing English-law documentation, L/C acceptance and clear dispute resolution.

Step 2

Foshan Sourcing

Our Foshan team selects the right factory for each product, negotiates pricing, consolidates mixed containers and manages production scheduling.

Step 3

QC & Shipping

Pre-shipment inspection with photo reports, container loading supervision, and full export documentation (CE, SGS, CoO, packing list).
