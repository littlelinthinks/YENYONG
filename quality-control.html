Quality Control & Testing

Three-stage inspection, verified test reports and pre-shipment photo documentation on every order.

Quality is not an afterthought at YENYONG. — it is built into every stage of the supply chain. From raw material verification to container loading supervision, our Foshan QC team ensures that what you order is what you receive.

Three-Stage QC Process

1

Incoming Material Inspection

Raw materials (SPC compound, PVC film, MDF substrate, aluminium coil) are verified against specification before production begins. Batch numbers are recorded for traceability.

2

In-Process Monitoring

Production lines are checked at regular intervals for dimensional accuracy, surface finish, lamination quality and colour consistency. Deviations are corrected before they become batch issues.

3

Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI)

Before packing, finished goods undergo AQL-level sampling: dimensions, thickness, surface quality, colour matching, packaging integrity. A photo report is sent to the client for approval before the container is loaded.

Testing Capabilities

Fire Performance

GB 8624-2012 and EN 13501-1 testing through SGS and CTI-accredited laboratories. Bamboo crystal panels verified at B1 (C-s2,d0) and EN B-s3,d0.

Formaldehyde & VOC

E1 and CARB Phase 2 compliance testing for wood-based panels. SVHC screening per EU REACH regulation.

Dimensional Stability

Thickness, width, length and squareness measured to ±0.2mm tolerance. Water absorption and thermal expansion tested for wet-area products.

Abrasion & Wear

SPC flooring wear layer tested for abrasion resistance (rpm to failure) and scratch hardness.

Acoustic Performance

NRC (Noise Reduction Coefficient) tested in reverberation chamber for acoustic panel products.

Load & Impact

Impact resistance, point load and indentation testing for flooring and high-traffic panels.
