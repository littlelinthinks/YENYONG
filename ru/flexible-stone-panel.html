Обзор изделия

Гибкие каменные панели - это ультратонкие, легкие виниры из натурального камня, которые изгибаются до изогнутых поверхностей. Реальная минеральная текстура с возможностью установки панели - идеально подходит для облицовки стен, колонн, потолков и мебели.

Ключевые особенности

Flexible & Bendable

Thin enough to wrap around columns, arches and curved walls. No more cutting stone into small pieces for curved surfaces.

Real Mineral Texture

Authentic travertine, woven and romanite surfaces — not a printed imitation. Each panel has natural variation.

Ultra-lightweight

3–6 mm thickness and low weight reduce structural load and shipping cost compared to solid stone.

Large Format

Panels up to 3000 × 1200 mm minimize seams and create a continuous stone surface.

Installation

Substrate

Install over flat, dry, stable substrate — plywood, cement board or drywall. Surface must be clean and free of dust.

Adhesive

Use flexible stone adhesive or neutral-cure silicone. Apply evenly with a trowel. Press firmly and support until cured.

Cutting

Cut with a sharp utility knife or heavy-duty scissors. Score and snap for straight cuts. Use a jigsaw for curved cuts.

Curved Surfaces

Panels can bend to a minimum radius of approximately 30 cm. Heat gently with a heat gun for tighter curves.
